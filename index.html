About

I'm in my final semester of a Computer Science and Engineering degree, standing at the intersection of design and development — a place most CSE students don't visit. Where a typical developer thinks in logic first, I think in layout, color, and story first, then build the system to support it.

My design toolkit runs through Midjourney and Google Flow, where I explore visual concepts before a single line of code exists. My development toolkit runs through C and C++, the languages my degree is built on, and Python, which I use for faster prototyping and my growing interest in computer vision.

That combination has shaped three real projects so far: a tourism website for Uttar Pradesh, a full business site for UMA Hair & Beauty Spa, and ExamMate, an AI-powered exam-prep tool built on the Gemini API. Each one needed both a designer's eye and a developer's hands — which is exactly the kind of work I want to keep doing.
